# Test audio

`fleurs-en_us-1904.wav` is utterance 1904 of the en_us test split of [FLEURS](https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/fleurs) by Google, licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), converted to 16 kHz mono PCM16 and levelled as `scripts/eval-stt.js` prepares its corpus. Its reference text is "However, due to the slow communication channels, styles in the west could lag behind by 25 to 30 year." The end-to-end tests play it as the microphone.
